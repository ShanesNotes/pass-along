01 · The front door

Today the homepage offers two doors. A guided path converts better.

First-time visitors have to choose between Pass and Find before they know what either means. The pattern that works everywhere else — Angi, Zocdoc, Thumbtack — is one question plus a ZIP code, then a short guided path. Same idea, in Pass Along’s voice.

Today · live site
Pass along
Find help
  • Two equal CTAs — no default action for a first visit
  • No location capture, so results can’t be local
  • No preview of what happens after the click
Step 1 of 3

Every step is skippable, and “just tell us in your own words” stays one tap away throughout — the guided path and free-text search feed the same engine. Recommenders get the mirror version: one question, then the story field.

02 · Find

Today: a text box. Proposed: search that understands it.

The find page already asks people to describe what’s going on in their own words — but nothing is wired behind it. The proposal: read the text for meaning, match it against passed-along stories, and explain each result in the recommender’s words. Try a sample, then tap an × in the “we heard” line to correct it.

Try:
Before anything else — we’re glad you told us. What you wrote sounds heavy, and you deserve support right now, not search results. If you’re in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out — someone is there around the clock.
Call or text 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Sample data; providers and quotes are fictional. In production, search text is read once and never stored, and three independent safety checks run before anything else.

03 · Pass

Today: stories land in a form. Proposed: each one is protected and put to work.

When a recommendation is submitted, three things should happen automatically: identifying details come out, searchable structure goes in, and the provider’s license gets checked. Press the button to watch.

A sample story is prefilled.

🔒 Anonymous, as always. No account, no name.

04 · Scope

A fixed core, modular options.

The core is non-negotiable: understanding, matching, safety, verification. Toggle options to sketch a release — each is small and independently shippable.

05 · The plan

Ten weeks, three phases

Small, safe, each phase shippable on its own. None is a rebuild.

Phase 1
Weeks 1–2
AI infrastructure

Database, submission pipeline, safety screening, and license-check plumbing stood up. Nothing visible changes yet.

Phase 2
Weeks 3–6
Search & onboarding

The guided front door and semantic search go live: understanding, matching, and “why this fits” lines quoting recommenders.

Phase 3
Weeks 7–10
Compounding trust

Verified badges with receipts, freshness check-ins, and the loops that make the corpus grow itself.
