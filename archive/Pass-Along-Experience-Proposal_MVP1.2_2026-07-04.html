Non-negotiables

Careful by design, because this is mental health.

🛟
Support before search

Every message passes three independent safety checks tuned so the one unacceptable failure is a miss. Anyone struggling sees warmth and 988 first — always, on both flows.

🔒
Privacy beyond the law

What people type while looking for help is read once and never stored, logged, or sold. No ad trackers on sensitive pages. Built to a stricter standard than regulation currently demands — because the standard is coming.

🌿
Search help, never advice

The engine only surfaces what the community said. It never assesses, diagnoses, or recommends treatment — which keeps the product warm, honest, and cleanly on the right side of the new wave of AI-and-mental-health laws.
